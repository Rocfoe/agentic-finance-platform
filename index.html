Hard boundaries

Capability is not authority. Every consequential operation resolves through identity, role, permission, policy, workflow step, and execution context.

  • Simulation: allowed as a controlled execution mode.
  • External side effects: explicit review boundary.
  • Financial execution: no autonomous trade, fund movement, withdrawal, or transfer in the current release.
  • Private memory: owner-scoped and not implicitly shared.
  • Promotion: requires explicit acceptance evidence.
